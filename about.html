Our Story

Passion for light.
Obsession for detail.

Chimra PixelCraft Studio was born from a simple but powerful belief — every moment, every face, every landscape deserves to be remembered beautifully. Founded and operated by Muntazir Ali from Satellite Town, Rawalpindi, the studio has grown from a personal passion into a trusted photography brand.

From the stormy skies of Rawalpindi to the towering peaks of Gilgit-Baltistan, our lens has traveled across Pakistan capturing its raw and stunning beauty. We bring the same dedication to weddings, portraits, and nature photography.

Our work is defined by honesty — real light, real emotion, real moments. No over-editing, no fake poses. Just genuine photography that stands the test of time.
